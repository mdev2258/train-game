# Right Time Railway

A single-file mobile game: run a real UK train operating company (SWR, Southern, GWR, LNER, Avanti, Northern, ScotRail or Merseyrail), buy new or second-hand rolling stock, keep PPM up and the DfT off your back.

- Open `index.html` in any browser (works best on a phone). No build step.
- Progress saves in the browser's local storage.
- Every 28-day rail period the DfT reviews PPM and satisfaction. Three warnings and Great British Railways takes over.
- Trains must match the power supply: third rail (DC), 25 kV overhead (AC), or diesel/bi-mode anywhere.

Unofficial fan game. Not affiliated with any operator. Fleet, route and money figures are approximate.
